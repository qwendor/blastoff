HOW TO PLAY
WASD move   MOUSE aim   SPACE jump, again in air for a double jump
LMB punch: tap 3 times for jab, jab, launching kick   Z lock-on (cycles targets)
RMB hold to charge a heavy swing, release to launch them   in the air: dive kick that spikes downward
SHIFT dash / dodge (invincible during the dash)   punch during a dash = tackle   Q rocket boost up
E shockwave on the ground, or a slam from the air that shockwaves on landing   TAB scores
PADS yellow pads launch you high   ORBS wings (+jumps), speed, shield, heal
🎮 controller: sticks move/aim, A jump, RT punch, LT heavy, X dash, Y boost, B slam, RB lock-on
🌐 online: host stays in the lobby, friend enters the code. Stuck connecting? Same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot fixes strict networks
Soundtrack is a built-in phonk beat (M mutes). Put a music.mp3 next to the game file to play your own track instead.
There's no health. Hits raise KNOCK %, and knockback scales with it. Fall below the arena and you're KO'd. Most KOs when time runs out wins. Falling with nobody to blame costs a point.
